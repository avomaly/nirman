SPECIFICATIONS // FAQ

LOGISTICS & ARCHIVE CARE

GLOBAL COURIER & DISPATCH +

All capsules are dispatched from our Tokyo and Berlin ateliers via carbon-neutral express courier. Orders over $200 receive complimentary express tracking. Customs duties and import fees are pre-calculated at checkout with zero clearance delays.

ARCHIVE RETURN PROTOCOL +

Due to the limited 250-unit nature of each release, items may be returned within 14 days of delivery in pristine, unwashed condition with all original security ribbons and numbered certificates attached.

SIZING & METRIC TOLERANCES +

Garments feature an intentional oversized architectural silhouette. Select your standard true-to-size fit for the intended runway drape, or choose one size down for a closer, tailored streetwear posture.

GARMENT CARE & FABRIC PRESERVATION +

Wash heavyweight 480GSM loopback fleece inside out in cold water (30°C max) using mild detergent. Air dry flat away from direct sunlight. Do not tumble dry. Iron garments on the reverse face to protect technical finishes.
